About Aaron

"You don't want to do research because it's distracting, but at the same time, I want the details."

Aaron Sorkin is an acclaimed screenwriter, director, and producer known for his distinctive writing style and sharp dialogue.

His famous works include The West Wing, The Social Network, and Steve Jobs.

Born on June 9, 1961, in New York City, Sorkin developed a passion for storytelling at an early age.
